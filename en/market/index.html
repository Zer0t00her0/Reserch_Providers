Glossary · 1 of 2 — Slots

Mechanics terms you will see ahead

RTP

Share of stakes a slot returns to players over the long run. 96% means for every 100 staked, players get back 96 on average

Volatility

How unevenly a slot pays. High means wins are rare but big

Max win, 15,000x

Maximum possible win in stakes: 15,000 times the bet

Multiplier

A symbol or effect that multiplies a win: 1000x means a thousand times

Bonus buy

Buying the bonus round outright at a fixed price, with no waiting

Tumble, cascades

Winning symbols vanish and new ones drop in — one spin can give several wins in a row

Scatter-pays

No lines: matching symbols pay anywhere on the grid if there are enough of them

Clusters

A group of matching symbols that touch each other pays

“1000” edition

A reissue of a Pragmatic hit with multipliers up to 1000x (the original Gates of Olympus went up to 500x)

Super Scatter

A special Pragmatic symbol: 1–4 of them instantly pay from 100x to 50,000x

GoO in tables is short for Gates of Olympus

Mechanics glossary. The 1000 ceiling: Gates of Olympus’s multiplier rose from 500x to 1000x, Sugar Rush’s from 128x to 1024x; since October 2023, 16 titles in the 1000 series have been released (OLBG). In 2025 Super Scatter rolled out across all Pragmatic franchises: Gates of Olympus in April, Mahjong Wins in June, Sweet Bonanza in August, then Sugar Rush and Starlight Princess.

Glossary · 2 of 2 — Market and data

Money, metrics and formats

GGR, revenue

Operator revenue: stakes minus payouts to players. This is how country markets are measured

Bet count

How many times a slot was spun. Shows mass play on small bets

Amount wagered

How much money was staked on a slot. Here VIP players with big bets decide

Lobby visibility

Blask estimate: how many casinos carry a game and how high they place it. A casino choice, not a player one

Interest share

Share of search interest — a Blask model estimate of how many people search for a game. Not money

Crash games

Fast games like Aviator: a multiplier climbs and the player must cash out before the “crash”

Originals

A casino’s own simple games: Dice, Plinko and the like

Sweepstakes

US “social” casinos on virtual coins; states increasingly ban them

Offshore

Casinos without a local licence that accept a country’s players

Q2, H1, FY

2nd quarter (April–June), 1st half-year, company financial year

All growth percentages in this deck are year on year unless stated otherwise

Glossary of market terms. Blask’s methodology is not disclosed, so visibility and interest share are model values. Stake.us is Stake’s US sweepstakes site, not Stake.com.

08 — Market structure

The market is moving to three poles

Mass market

Pragmatic franchises

Over 100 new games in 2025 (up to 8 slots a month), the 1000 and Super Scatter series, 39 industry awards.

First for lobby visibility in almost every country and for bet count on Stake.

For high-risk players

Hacksaw, Nolimit City, ELK

Slots with rare but big wins and a recognisable visual style, spun by streamers on Kick.

On Stake their games rank higher by amount wagered than by bet count: they are played with big bets.

The casino makes its own games

Stake ecosystem

Juice (Sept 2026): five studios that made games only for Stake. Reportedly over $12bn has been wagered on their games to date.

Stake Engine: third-party studios publish games directly on Stake and give up 10% of their revenue.

Sources: Pragmatic Play, NEXT.io, Casino.org, Sharpr

The content market is stratifying. Pragmatic wins on volume and franchises, high-risk studios (high volatility) on style and streamers, and Stake turns an operator into a content producer. Juice: Twist, Knucklehead Syndicate, Monstrums, Massive Studios, The Originals; the plan is one slot and two house games a week. Juice already sells games to other operators, Roobet being the first.

11 — Regulation

Taxes decide who grows

Moderate tax

+17%

Spain: online gambling revenue growth in 2025 with a 20% tax

Denmark, with a 28% tax, is also up 12%. Slots generate 69% of online casino revenue in Spain and 82% in Denmark.

High tax and restrictions

−18%

Netherlands: online casino revenue decline in 2025 with a 37.8% tax

Germany: max €1 stake per spin, 5 seconds between spins, 5.3% tax on every stake — an estimated 70–80% of play goes to unlicensed casinos.

What it means: in Europe, moderate tax goes hand in hand with legal market growth, while high tax plus restrictions goes with players moving offshore. When choosing a launch country, look at both factors.

Sources: DGOJ, Spillemyndigheden, KSA, GGL, CDC Gaming

The European pattern: moderate taxes go with double-digit growth; high taxes with product restrictions go with falling legal revenue and leakage offshore. The Dutch regulator itself admitted the tax increase brought no additional revenue. Since July 2026 Germany has had a €3/€5 limit option.

12 — Mechanics · 1 of 2

The 2025–2026 hit formula: four elements

01

Lineless grid and cascades

Groups of matching symbols pay anywhere on the grid; winning symbols vanish and new ones drop in

02

Multipliers that build up

The ceiling has risen: Gates of Olympus from 500x to 1000x, Sugar Rush from 128x to 1024x

03

Bonus buy

All 10 of Stake’s most-played slots have it (April–June 2025)

04

Huge max win

Usually 10,000–50,000x the stake; the record is 500,000x in Tombstone Slaughter (Nolimit City)

What it means: the race for ever-bigger max wins is hitting a ceiling. According to executives at Big Time Gaming, Spribe and 1X2 Network, games will now stand out through engaging base play, visible progress toward a big win and social mechanics

Sources: NEXT.io, OLBG, The Bet Press round table

Pragmatic’s two key innovations: the 1000 ceiling (16 titles in the series since October 2023) and Super Scatter — one to four of these symbols instantly pay from 100x to 50,000x. In October 2026 a typical new release caps at 10,000x. Spribe’s CPO: “the potential for a big win alone is no longer enough.” 1X2 Network’s CPO proposed syncing sound and visuals with the game’s mathematical state.

13 — Players and streamers

They play the same games they slam

Trustpilot reviews

1.1 of 5

Pragmatic’s average rating (about 1,200 reviews, ~95% one star); Hacksaw has 1.2. Complaints: “rigged” maths and dead spins.

What it means: lots of negativity signals wide reach, not necessarily a worse game.

Reduced RTP

92% not 96%

Because of taxes, many UK casinos run slot versions that return about 92% of stakes to players rather than the expected 96%.

Players notice it as longer losing streaks — their most justified complaint.

Streamers on Kick

$45.4m

won by streamer Roshtein in July 2025 on $10,000 bets — reigniting the debate over whether streamer balances are real money.

Streamers mostly play Hacksaw and Nolimit City.

Sources: Trustpilot, iGamingToday, Traffic Cardinal. Reddit and Russian-language forums gave little primary data

Players vote in two ways — with play volume and with reviews — and the same providers are on top in both. Nolimit City, Hacksaw and ELK have cult status. “Book of” clones and fake Pragmatic slots on fraudulent sweepstakes sites have a bad reputation. Streamers’ picks (Trainwreckstv, xQc, Roshtein) match Stake’s ranking by amount wagered, not by bet count.

16 — Design takeaways

Slot UX: what designers should watch

01

Portrait screen, one hand

About 80% of ELK’s play is on smartphones. Spin button in thumb reach, tall reels, menus in the corners, fast loading.

02

Readable on stream

A game must read in a small stream window and in thumbnails. Wanted Dead or a Wild is valued for being easy for viewers to follow; Hacksaw is praised for “clean design”.

03

Visible progress

A growing multiplier, meters and stages to the bonus; sound and visuals change with the game state so players know where they are. This is now the key differentiator.

04

Studio style as brand

Pragmatic’s bright mass market vs the “graphic novel” look of Nolimit and Hacksaw. Characters as IP (ELK), seasonal waves, viewer bets on bonuses during streams (EXCO AMP).

Sources: OLBG (ELK interview), Moss and Fog, win.gg, The Bet Press

Four design takeaways. Mobile portrait format by default, readability on stream, visualising progress instead of chasing max win, and a recognisable studio visual language. EXCO AMP lets viewers place prediction bets on bonuses during a stream.

Conclusion

A slot’s popularity depends on where you measure it

Competitor lobbies show what they put on display; search shows what people talk about. Only spins and bets in your own casino show what players pay for.

Growth areas

Markets opening or growing under moderate taxes: Alberta, Finland (2027), New Zealand, Spain, Italy and legal Brazil

The main risk in building a lobby is relying on a single ranking. Competitive edge for a new product: fast per-country lobby localisation, in-house fast games (originals or crash) as a retention layer, and UX built around readable progress and streamability.
